Nandu,

We've never actually spent time together in person. No trips, no hangouts, not a single photo of us to prove any of this happened. And somehow you're still one of the people I feel closest to. That has to say something.

It was built entirely out of late-night chats — the kind where neither of us was actually tired, we just didn't want to hang up. Out of laughing at things that weren't even that funny, just because it was you. Out of me venting about career tension I couldn't say out loud to anyone else, and you actually listening instead of just waiting for your turn to talk. Out of family pressure we both carried and somehow made lighter just by saying it out loud to someone who got it.

Even our little arguments feel like proof of something — you don't fight with people you don't care about. We never needed a shared place or a shared photo. We just needed the chats. That's more than enough.

Always your Mr Best Friend 🤍
